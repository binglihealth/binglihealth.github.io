About

I am a cross-disciplinary scientist and entrepreneur at the forefront of Medical-Engineering Integration, currently leading the technological vision at Thuspine (backed by Tsinghua University, the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and Peking University Third Hospital).

My mission is to build the "Scale AI" for human surface physiological data — transforming how we decode, analyze, and intervene in human movement and chronic pain.

Before co-founding Thuspine, I dedicated my career to bridging next-generation flexible materials with domain-specific AI models. I believe the next frontier of AI is not just about computing power, but about capturing the "first-principle data" of human biology through seamless, high-fidelity wearable technologies — proving that superior AI requires equally superior hardware as its data entrance.

Research & Focus Areas
Four pillars that span fundamental material science to clinical RCT validation.

Next-Gen Wearable Sensing

BCI signal acquisition and conformal electrodes — enabling dynamic, motion-artifact-free, long-term biopotential recording.

Domain-Specific AI (Vertical AI)

Multi-modal deep learning (GNNs + Transformers) and reinforcement learning for personalized, real-time healthcare intervention.

Closed-loop Digital Therapeutics

End-to-end ecosystem: AI Hardware → APP Analytics → Cloud Clinical Decision Support for musculoskeletal (MSK) diseases.

Human Data Infrastructure

Standardizing sEMG & kinematic data to empower biological AI (clinical rehab) and physical AI (humanoid robots & HCI).
