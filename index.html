// About

I am a cross-disciplinary scientist and entrepreneur working at the intersection of flexible electronics, machine learning, and clinical rehabilitation. I currently lead the technology vision at Thuspine, a venture supported by a collaborative research network spanning Tsinghua University, the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Hong Kong Polytechnic University, and Peking University Third Hospital.

My work focuses on a simple observation: medicine happens inside hospitals, but life — and relapse — happens outside. Closing that gap requires capturing first-principle biological data from the body surface, in daily living, with clinical-grade fidelity.

Across platforms I go by binglihealth (LinkedIn · GitHub). In academic and industry contexts in China, I am 李冰 (Li Bing).

// Research & Focus

Four pillars, one data pipeline

01

Next-generation wearable sensing

Semi-liquid-metal flexible electrodes and conformal structures — pursuing clinical-grade signal quality, long-term comfort, and dynamic use cases simultaneously (the "impossible triangle" of electrode design).

02

Vertical AI for biosignals

Multi-modal sEMG modeling with GNNs and Transformers: cross-user zero-calibration and drift-adaptive inference — models that work out of the box and improve with use.

03

Closed-loop digital therapeutics

A monitor → alert → intervene → prove methodology. Complementing clinicians, never replacing them — with intervention delivered before symptoms escalate.

04

Human data infrastructure

Standardized sEMG data dimensions and protocols — serving clinical research today, embodied intelligence and HCI tomorrow.
